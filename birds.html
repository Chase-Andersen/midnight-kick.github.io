Birds

Caring for Your Bird’s Emotional Health

Birds are fascinating creatures, ranging in size from the tiny Cuban bee hummingbird (just two inches long and slightly over 1/20 of an ounce) to the massive ostrich. While most birds can fly, some species cannot. Despite their differences, every bird is unique in its relational needs and overall well-being.

Unfortunately, birds can experience sadness and depression much like humans. Common causes may include:

Physical or mental illness. Losing a bonded companion. Prolonged boredom or lack of stimulation.

Some signs your bird may be experiencing depression include:

Reduced appetite. Increased irritability. Aggressive behavior. Unusual changes in songs or calls (more solemn in tone).

If you notice any of these symptoms, consult your veterinarian. As with many illnesses, early identification and treatment can greatly improve your bird’s quality of life and longevity.
