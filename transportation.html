Transportation on Taniti Island

Arriving on the Island

Almost all visitors arrive to Taniti by air, though a small cruise ship docks at Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week. The island is served by a small airport that accommodates small jets and propeller planes. Plans are underway to expand the airport so that larger jets will be able to land within the next few years.

Public & Private Transport

Public buses operate throughout Taniti City daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., while private buses serve destinations across the rest of the island. Taxis are available in Taniti City, and rental cars can be arranged at a local agency near the airport.

Biking & Walking

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors, and helmets are required by law. Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, making it easy for tourists to explore on foot. Many visitors stay around Merriton Landing, an area that is particularly easy to navigate by walking or biking.

Tips for Getting Around

Visitors are encouraged to plan transportation in advance for trips outside Taniti City. Public transport and taxis are reliable for city travel, while bikes and walking are ideal for short distances and exploring the local neighborhoods.
